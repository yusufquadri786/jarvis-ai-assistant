04 / If You Suspect a Compromise

Start with safe recovery steps rather than trying to retaliate or break into another device.

PHONE • Update the OS and remove unfamiliar apps. • Review account sign-in sessions and revoke unknown sessions. • Change important account passwords from a trusted device. • Enable multi-factor authentication. • Contact the device maker or a trusted technician if concerns continue. WI-FI • Change router admin and Wi-Fi passwords. • Install official firmware updates. • Remove unknown devices in router settings. • Contact your ISP/router maker if you cannot secure it.
